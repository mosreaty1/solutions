Featured Project

PointBus

A Mobile Transit Platform for New Alamein City

New Alamein City suffers from severe internal bus traffic chaos — overcrowded vehicles, zero digital tracking, no advance booking, and passengers with no idea when the next bus arrives.

PointBus solves Alamein's bus traffic chaos — letting residents search, book, and track bus journeys in real time, ending overcrowding and long waits.

The Problem We Solved

🚌

Overcrowded Buses

Buses fill beyond capacity at peak hours — no system limits or tracks seat availability.

⏱

No Real-Time Tracking

Passengers wait at stops with no idea when the next bus arrives or which route it serves.

🎟

No Booking System

Boarding is chaotic and first-come first-served — no reservation, no queue, no order.

📵

Zero Digital Tools

Zero digital tracking or management — entirely offline with no data or analytics available.

App Features

What Makes PointBus Different?

01

Real-Time Route Search

Find buses between any 2 of 24 stops across 16 lines instantly. Start and end point selection with live availability.

02

Explore the Map

Google Maps live view with animated moving buses and location tracking across all active routes.

03

Seat Selection

Visual 40-seat bus map with occupied/available states. Choose your exact seat and confirm your booking instantly.

04

Digital Booking & Ticket

Confirm your trip with a digital electronic ticket — no cash, no paper, no queues. Built for environmental conservation.

05

Account Balance & Top-Up Cards

Physical top-up cards with QR codes let users add money to their digital wallet instantly. Monitor your spending directly in-app.
